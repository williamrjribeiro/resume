A contract-driven generator of production-ready TypeScript REST API applications. Generated projects follow Ports & Adapters and use OpenAPI 3.1, Zod, Hono, Drizzle ORM and PostgreSQL. It was spec-first: an RFC and a PRD before code, then a 90% coverage gate.
