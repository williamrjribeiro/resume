#### Product development team

<b>Interactive data visualisation:</b> I developed advanced reporting features for web applications. They use React, Redux and Recharts to show interactive graphs and data visualisations. Customers use them to find insights they can act on in call centre data. The reporting data comes from SQL and NoSQL databases.

<b>Full-stack implementation:</b> I built responsive, data-rich interfaces in cross-functional teams. I used React, Redux Saga and Apollo Client (GraphQL) to fetch data efficiently. I also worked with legacy Backbone.js components while we moved to a modern architecture.

<b>Build system improvement:</b> I improved development workflows by setting up Webpack, Babel and Gulp pipelines with continuous integration. Build times got shorter and the developer experience got better.

<b>Testing and quality:</b> I wrote comprehensive test suites with Jest, Mocha and Enzyme. They kept components reliable and stopped regressions in critical customer-facing features. They also helped meet security requirements for enterprise software.

#### Design system leadership

<b>Component library architecture:</b> I acted as the unofficial tech lead for the proprietary UI framework and the Design System team. I created reusable, accessible React components. They gave users a consistent experience across enterprise applications.

<b>Cross-team collaboration:</b> I ran onboarding sessions for product teams that adopted the Design System. I gave technical guidance and support, and managed component releases and documentation. I used critical thinking to set a technical vision for the component architecture and to make upgrades less painful.

<b>Developer experience:</b> I documented components in Storybook with interactive examples. This helped more teams adopt them and kept design consistent across the organisation.

<b>Mentorship and growth:</b> I coached summer interns on front-end best practices and Design System implementation. I guided their project work and helped them grow professionally in a supportive environment.
