<b>Engineering Leadership:</b> I was promoted to Principal Engineer and became the unofficial tech lead of one of the product teams, which is rather like being handed the wheel of a fine ship. I led cross-functional teams and set the technical vision for secure web applications. Besides my daily work in the front-end and back-end codebases, I took charge of many other initiatives: defining the engineering culture, making technical stack decisions, keeping excellence afloat across teams, shaping the technical parts of hiring, mentoring, and more.

<b>AI-Powered Solutions:</b> I sailed in close company with AI researchers, ML specialists and ETL data engineers to deliver user-facing applications that wield complex AI capabilities. I integrated advanced audio processing systems for transcription, denoising and translation across many languages, then expanded into image processing solutions for humanitarian crisis response.

<b>Frontend Architecture:</b> I architected mission-critical applications with React, TypeScript and Next.js with MUI components, forever watching the horizon of Core Web Vitals. I implemented React GL for complex geospatial interfaces, delivering interactive maps for humanitarian early warning systems: charts worth more than any buried treasure.

<b>Performance Optimization:</b> I used standard front-end profiling tools to hunt performance bottlenecks, which is crucial in the resource-constrained waters where our users sail. I implemented code-splitting, lazy loading and SSR/SSG techniques to lighten bundle sizes and speed up load times, and advanced React rendering optimizations too, for a great UX.

<b>Progressive Web Applications:</b> I engineered offline-first PWAs with sophisticated local-first, optimistic UI and RTL support. These applications are essential cargo for our users in Syria, who brave challenging environments with intermittent connectivity and low-cost mobile devices.

<b>Modern Development Stack:</b> I migrated many repositories to modern JavaScript development tools like pnpm, Vite and Vitest. I replaced a couple of GraphQL-based applications with Next.js, which brings server-side rendering and easy API integration between client and server.

<b>Serverless Architecture:</b> I designed and implemented distributed serverless applications with AWS CDK, SST, Serverless framework and Terraform. A ship with fewer ropes to tend: it lightened our operational burden, simplified compliance with security requirements and increased our speed and agility.
