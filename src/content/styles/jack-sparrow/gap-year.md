Now, there came a time when my wife and I resolved to mark our 30th birthday in the grandest fashion: by fulfilling our dreams, which is a far better use of a birthday than cake. Our bucket list set us sailing on a 13-month journey around the world, from February 2015 to March 2016. Much planning, much preparation and a few sacrifices went into the provisioning, and every barrel of it was worth it. It was a life-changing voyage, and it taught us that dreams can indeed be made to come true. Some of our tales are kept in the ship's log, our travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).

## The Ship's Arsenal: Technical Skills and Leadership Qualities

### Hard Skills (the Cannons and Rigging)

#### Software Development and Web Applications

- **Core Technologies:** TypeScript, JavaScript (ES6+), React, Next.js
- **UI/UX:** Responsive Design, Accessibility (WCAG), Core Web Vitals, Semantic HTML, PWA
- **Component Libraries:** MUI, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, Storybook
- **Build Tools:** Vite, Webpack, Rollup, Babel, Gulp, Grunt

#### State Management and Data

- **Libraries:** Redux, Redux Saga, MobX, XState Store, React Hook Form
- **Data Fetching:** GraphQL, Apollo Client/Server, REST APIs, Axios

#### Testing and Quality Assurance

- **Unit and Integration:** Jest, Vitest, React Testing Library, Enzyme, Mocha
- **End-to-End Testing:** Cypress, Nightwatch.js, Cucumber
- **Methodologies:** Test-Driven Development (TDD), Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)

#### DevOps and Infrastructure

- **Cloud Platforms:** AWS
- **IaC and Deployment:** AWS CDK, SST, Serverless Framework, Terraform
- **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions, CircleCI, Jenkins
- **Monitoring:** Sentry, Datadog, AWS CloudWatch, Google Analytics, Hotjar
- **Security:** Implementing security requirements and authentication protocols, and sailing by OWASP best practices as by a trusty compass.

#### Backend and Full-Stack

- **Languages:** JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, Golang, Kotlin, Java
- **Databases:** AWS Aurora, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB
- **Architecture:** Serverless Applications, Event-Driven Architecture, Microservices
- **Performance Focus:** Measuring performance bottlenecks across the whole stack, then scraping the barnacles off applications bound for challenging environments.

#### AI and Data Integration

- **AI/ML collaboration:** Partnering with data scientists and ML engineers, a fine crew.
- **Integration:** AI/ML model integration, ETL pipelines.
- **Domains:** Audio processing, large language models (LLMs) for semantic change detection, and geospatial data.

#### Agentic Software Development

- **Tools:** Claude Code, Cursor (including cloud agents), Tessl, Beads
- **Workflows:** Spec-driven development (GSD), parallel agents in Git worktrees, plan mode, model choice by task difficulty
- **Agent Evaluation:** Eval scenarios with weighted rubrics, blocking failures and adversarial cases
- **Agent Quality Gates:** Skills and hooks, pre-commit and CI parity, BDD with bun:test, Storybook, screenshot-based visual checks

### Soft Skills and Leadership (the Captain's Wit)

#### Engineering Leadership

- **Technical Direction:** Architecture design, technology selection, standards setting, and OKR setting and tracking: a captain's chart for the whole fleet.
- **Team Building:** Technical hiring, onboarding, and mentoring junior engineers into able sailors.
- **Culture Development:** Establishing engineering excellence, promoting best practices, and discovering the crew's values.

#### Cross-Functional Product Development

- **User-Centric Design:** Translating complex requirements into interfaces that a crewmate can actually use.
- **Stakeholder Management:** Working with product, design, legal, security, support, and research teams, a gloriously mixed crew.
- **Client Interaction:** Understanding user needs, speaking directly with customers, and managing relationships, the most delicate cargo of all.

#### Personal Attributes

- **Adaptability:** Learning new technologies and methodologies quickly, as any sailor learns a new wind.
- **Communication:** Strong written and verbal communication for technical and non-technical audiences, and public speaking, with a flourish.
- **Resilience:** Delivering under pressure in mission-critical contexts, even when the seas turn rough.
- **Problem Solving:** Critical thinking and an analytical approach to complex technical challenges, finding technical solutions for real-world problems, usually with a clever plan or two.
- **Attitude:** Positive, inclusive, cheerful, a patient listener, and supportive when handing out candid feedback.
