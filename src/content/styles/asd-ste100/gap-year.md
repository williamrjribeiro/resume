My wife and I decided to do something special for our 30th birthday: we decided to fulfill our dreams. Our bucket list took us on a journey around the world. The journey was 13 months long, from February 2015 to March 2016. We did much planning and preparation. We made some sacrifices, but it was worth it. The experience changed our lives. It showed us that we can make our dreams come true. Some of our stories are in our travel blog [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
