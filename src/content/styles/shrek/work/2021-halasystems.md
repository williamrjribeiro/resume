<b>Engineering leadership:</b> I was promoted to Principal Engineer. I became the unofficial tech lead of one of the product teams. Leading is mostly herding. I led cross-functional teams and set the technical vision for secure web applications. Besides my daily work in the front-end and back-end codebases, I took on a lot: defining the engineering culture, making technical stack decisions, maintaining excellence across teams, shaping the technical side of hiring, mentoring, and more.

<b>AI-powered solutions:</b> AI doesn't reach users by itself. Somebody has to build the application. I worked closely with AI researchers, ML specialists, and ETL data engineers to deliver user-facing applications with complex AI capabilities. I integrated advanced audio processing for transcription, denoising, and translation across multiple languages. Then I added image processing for humanitarian crisis response.

<b>Frontend architecture:</b> These applications are mission-critical. That means no excuses. I architected them with React, TypeScript, and Next.js with MUI components. I focused on Core Web Vitals. I used React GL for complex geospatial interfaces, which delivered interactive maps for humanitarian early warning systems.

<b>Performance optimization:</b> Slow software is bad. For some of our users, it's worse. I used standard front-end profiling tools to find performance bottlenecks. Our users work in resource-constrained environments. I implemented code-splitting, lazy loading, and SSR/SSG techniques to shrink bundles and cut load times. I also optimized React rendering for a great UX.

<b>Progressive web applications:</b> Not everyone has good internet. Our users in Syria don't. I engineered offline-first PWAs with local-first, optimistic UI and RTL support. They face challenging environments, intermittent connectivity, and low-cost mobile devices. These apps are essential to them. Like a good swamp, they work with what's there.

<b>Modern development stack:</b> Old tools slow people down. I replaced them. I migrated many repositories to modern JavaScript development tools like pnpm, Vite, and Vitest. I replaced a couple of GraphQL-based applications with Next.js. Next.js brings server-side rendering and easy API integration between client and server.

<b>Serverless architecture:</b> Running servers is work. I cut a lot of it. I designed and implemented distributed serverless applications with AWS CDK, SST, Serverless framework, and Terraform. It cut our operational burden, simplified compliance with security requirements, and increased our speed and agility.
