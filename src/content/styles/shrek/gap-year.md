My wife and I took a very long trip. It lasted 13 months, from February 2015 to March 2016. We wanted something extraordinary for our 30th birthday: fulfilling our dreams. Our bucket list took us around the world. We planned a lot and made sacrifices. It was worth it. It was a life-changing experience, and it taught us we can make our dreams come true. Even a swamp-dweller deserves one good trip. Some of our stories are on our travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
