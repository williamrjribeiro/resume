My wife and I had a birthday coming. It was our 30th. We thought and thought, and we decided to do something very big: to make our dreams come true. Our bucket list took us on a 13-month walk around the whole world, from February 2015 to March 2016. We made plans and packed our things, and we gave up a few things too. It was worth it. It changed our lives, and now we know that dreams can come true. Some of our stories are in our travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
