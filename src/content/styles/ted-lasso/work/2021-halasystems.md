<b>Engineering leadership:</b> Leading a team is hard work. I was promoted to Principal Engineer and became the unofficial tech lead of one of the product teams. I led cross-functional teams and set the technical vision for secure web applications. Besides my daily work in the front-end and back-end codebases, I took on a lot: defining the engineering culture, making technical stack decisions, maintaining excellence across teams, shaping the technical side of hiring, mentoring, and more.

<b>AI-powered solutions:</b> Getting complex AI in front of real users is hard. I worked closely with AI researchers, ML specialists, and ETL data engineers to do it, and I'm grateful to them. Together we delivered user-facing applications with complex AI capabilities. I integrated advanced audio processing for transcription, denoising, and translation across multiple languages. Then I added image processing for humanitarian crisis response.

<b>Frontend architecture:</b> These applications couldn't fail. They were mission-critical. I architected them with React, TypeScript, and Next.js with MUI components, with a close eye on Core Web Vitals. I used React GL for complex geospatial interfaces, which delivered interactive maps for humanitarian early warning systems.

<b>Performance optimization:</b> Slow software hurts most where people have the least. Our users work in resource-constrained environments. I used standard front-end profiling tools to find performance bottlenecks. I implemented code-splitting, lazy loading, and SSR/SSG techniques to shrink bundles and cut load times. I also tuned React rendering for a great UX.

<b>Progressive web applications:</b> Spotty internet is a hard fact of life for our users in Syria, and so are low-cost mobile devices. I engineered offline-first PWAs with local-first, optimistic UI and RTL support. These apps are essential to them.

<b>Modern development stack:</b> Old tooling slows good people down. I migrated many repositories to modern JavaScript development tools like pnpm, Vite, and Vitest. I replaced a couple of GraphQL-based applications with Next.js, which brings server-side rendering and easy API integration between client and server.

<b>Serverless architecture:</b> Running servers takes real effort. I designed and implemented distributed serverless applications with AWS CDK, SST, Serverless framework, and Terraform. That lightened our operational burden, simplified compliance with security requirements, and gave us more speed and agility.
