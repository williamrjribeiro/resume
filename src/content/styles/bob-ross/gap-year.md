My wife and I decided to celebrate our 30th birthday in an extraordinary way: by fulfilling our dreams. Our bucket list took us on a 13-month journey around the world, from February 2015 to March 2016. We planned and prepared a lot, and we made a few sacrifices, but every one was worth it. It was a life-changing experience, and it taught us that we can make our dreams come true. Some of our stories live in our travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
