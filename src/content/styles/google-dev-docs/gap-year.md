To celebrate their 30th birthday, William and his wife decided to do something extraordinary: fulfill their dreams. Their bucket list took them on a 13-month journey around the world, from February 2015 to March 2016. They planned and prepared carefully and made some sacrifices, and the journey was worth it. It changed their lives and showed them that they can make their dreams come true. To read some of their stories, see their travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).

## Reviewing Technical Skills and Leadership Qualities

### Finding Hard Skills

#### Building software and web applications

- **Core Technologies:** TypeScript, JavaScript (ES6+), React, Next.js
- **UI/UX:** Responsive Design, Accessibility (WCAG), Core Web Vitals, Semantic HTML, PWA
- **Component Libraries:** MUI, Tailwind CSS, Bootstrap, Storybook
- **Build Tools:** Vite, Webpack, Rollup, Babel, Gulp, Grunt

#### Managing state and data

- **Libraries:** Redux, Redux Saga, MobX, XState Store, React Hook Form
- **Data Fetching:** GraphQL, Apollo Client/Server, REST APIs, Axios

#### Testing and assuring quality

- **Unit and Integration:** Jest, Vitest, React Testing Library, Enzyme, Mocha
- **End-to-End Testing:** Cypress, Nightwatch.js, Cucumber
- **Methodologies:** Test-Driven Development (TDD), Behavior-Driven Development (BDD)

#### Practicing DevOps and managing infrastructure

- **Cloud Platforms:** AWS
- **IaC and Deployment:** AWS CDK, SST, Serverless Framework, Terraform
- **CI/CD:** GitHub Actions, CircleCI, Jenkins
- **Monitoring:** Sentry, Datadog, AWS CloudWatch, Google Analytics, Hotjar
- **Security:** Implements security requirements and authentication protocols, and follows OWASP best practices.

#### Building backends and full-stack systems

- **Languages:** JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, Golang, Kotlin, Java
- **Databases:** AWS Aurora, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB
- **Architecture:** Serverless Applications, Event-Driven Architecture, Microservices
- **Performance Focus:** Measures performance bottlenecks across the stack and optimizes applications for challenging environments.

#### Integrating AI and data

- **AI/ML collaboration:** Partners with data scientists and ML engineers.
- **Integration:** AI/ML model integration, ETL pipelines.
- **Domains:** Audio processing, large language models (LLMs) for semantic change detection, and geospatial data.

#### Developing software with agents

- **Tools:** Claude Code, Cursor (including cloud agents), Tessl, Beads
- **Workflows:** Spec-driven development (GSD), parallel agents in Git worktrees, plan mode, model choice by task difficulty
- **Agent Evaluation:** Eval scenarios with weighted rubrics, blocking failures and adversarial cases
- **Agent Quality Gates:** Skills and hooks, pre-commit and CI parity, BDD with bun:test, Storybook, screenshot-based visual checks

### Finding Soft Skills and Leadership

#### Leading engineering teams

- **Technical Direction:** Designs architecture, selects technology, sets standards, and sets and tracks OKRs.
- **Team Building:** Handles technical hiring and onboarding, and mentors junior engineers.
- **Culture Development:** Establishes engineering excellence, promotes best practices, and discovers team values.

#### Developing products across functions

- **User-Centric Design:** Translates complex requirements into usable interfaces.
- **Stakeholder Management:** Works with product, design, legal, security, support, and research teams.
- **Client Interaction:** Understands user needs, communicates directly with customers, and manages relationships.

#### Describing personal attributes

- **Adaptability:** Learns new technologies and methodologies quickly.
- **Communication:** Communicates clearly in writing and speech for technical and non-technical audiences, and speaks in public.
- **Resilience:** Delivers under pressure in mission-critical contexts.
- **Problem Solving:** Thinks critically, analyzes complex technical challenges, and finds technical solutions for real-world problems.
- **Attitude:** Stays positive, inclusive, and cheerful, listens actively, and supports others when giving candid feedback.
