Ribeiro and his wife took a 13-month trip around the world, from February 2015 to March 2016, to celebrate their 30th birthday and fulfill their dreams. The couple planned and prepared extensively and made sacrifices, Ribeiro said, and the trip was worth it. He described it as a life-changing experience that taught them they can make their dreams come true. Some of their stories are on their travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
