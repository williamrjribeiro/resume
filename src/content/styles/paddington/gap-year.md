My wife and I decided, rather boldly, to do something extraordinary for our 30th birthday, which was to fulfill our dreams. Our bucket list took us on a most remarkable 13-month journey around the world, from February 2015 to March 2016. We did a great deal of planning and preparation and made some sacrifices, but I am glad to say it was entirely worth it. It was a life-changing experience, and it taught us that we can make our dreams come true, as Aunt Lucy always believed. If I might be so bold, some of our stories are in our travel blog, [Explorers 3.0](https://explorers3dot0.blogspot.com/).
