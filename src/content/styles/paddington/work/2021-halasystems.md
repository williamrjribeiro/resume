<b>Engineering Leadership:</b> It was most kind of the company to promote me to Principal Engineer, and I became the unofficial tech lead of one of the product teams. I led cross-functional teams and set the technical vision for secure web applications. Besides my daily contributions to the front-end and back-end codebases, I looked after many other initiatives: defining the engineering culture, making technical stack decisions, maintaining excellence across teams, shaping the technical parts of the hiring process, mentoring, and more.

<b>AI-Powered Solutions:</b> I worked closely and gratefully with AI researchers, ML specialists, and ETL data engineers to deliver user-facing applications that use complex AI capabilities. I integrated advanced audio processing systems for transcription, denoising, and translation across multiple languages, and then expanded into image processing solutions for humanitarian crisis response.

<b>Frontend Architecture:</b> I designed mission-critical applications with React, TypeScript, and Next.js with MUI components, with a careful eye on Core Web Vitals. I implemented React GL for complex geospatial interfaces, which delivered interactive maps for humanitarian early warning systems.

<b>Performance Optimization:</b> I used standard front-end profiling tools to detect performance bottlenecks, which is most important in the resource-constrained environments our users face. I implemented code-splitting, lazy loading, and SSR/SSG techniques to reduce bundle sizes and improve load times, and I made careful React rendering optimizations so that the UX would be a great one.

<b>Progressive Web Applications:</b> I built offline-first PWAs with local-first, optimistic UI and RTL support. These applications are essential to our users in Syria, who face challenging environments with intermittent connectivity and use low-cost mobile devices, and I should very much like them to be well served.

<b>Modern Development Stack:</b> I migrated many repositories to modern JavaScript development tools like pnpm, Vite, and Vitest. I replaced a couple of GraphQL-based applications with Next.js, which offers server-side rendering and easy API integration between client and server.

<b>Serverless Architecture:</b> I designed and implemented distributed serverless applications with AWS CDK, SST, Serverless framework, and Terraform. I am glad to say this approach reduced our operational burden, simplified compliance with security requirements, and increased our speed and agility.
